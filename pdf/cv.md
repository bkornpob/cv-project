- **InjectPrompt Companion 3.0** | [companion.injectprompt.com](https://companion.injectprompt.com/) | Structured prompt engineering and systematic extraction methodologies for safety testing. Multi-tactic jailbreak templates following the Jailbreak Perturbation Framework (JPF). A progressive learning curriculum powering research from beginner through advanced levels.
- **hstgrism** | [pypi.org/project/hstgrism/](https://pypi.org/project/hstgrism/) | Python 3 package for reducing HST grism observations, providing a modular, aXe-compatible workflow with simpler installation, diagnostics, and flexible point-source extraction. Optimized for flexible point-source extraction and 2D polynomial background modeling. Powering data reduction pipelines in peer-reviewed observational studies, including [Bhirombhakdi et al. (2024)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024ApJ...977..256B/abstract).
